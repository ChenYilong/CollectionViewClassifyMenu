# SVGA sample files

Unmodified copies of CYLTabBarController's SVGA tab icons
(<https://github.com/ChenYilong/CYLTabBarController>, branch `feat/cross-platform-components`, commit `c6320222`,
path `Example-SwiftUI-Rive/AnimatedApp/SVGAAssets/`). Their origin, as CYLTabBarController's `NOTICE.md` records it:
[kongpf8848/Animation](https://github.com/kongpf8848/Animation) `app/src/main/assets/svga`, Apache License 2.0
(the repository's license); renamed after the tab they show. They are 96 × 96 tab icons that play once and keep
their last frame. Replace them if their origin is ever disputed.
